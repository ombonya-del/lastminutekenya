What we collect

When you request a rescue or a Site Assessment through LastMinuteKenya, we collect the details you provide: your name and contact number, the event location and time, the service category needed, and any notes about the situation. Service providers in the Last Minute Network give us their business details, service area, and capacity. Site Assessors record their on-site findings.

How we use it

We use your information solely to match you with capable providers, to dispatch and coordinate a last-minute intervention, to produce a Last Minute Scorecard and Report, and to send advisory alerts to nearby providers so the network is ready to help. We do not sell your data.

Advisory alerts

Where a request or a Site Assessment suggests an intervention may be needed, we may send location-based advisory alerts (email, WhatsApp or SMS) to relevant providers. These alerts describe the likely need and area — never your identity or full contact details until you choose to engage a provider.

Who can see your data

Personal details are protected by row-level security. Providers see only what they need to respond to a job. Assessors see only their assigned visits. The public anonymous key used by this app cannot read personal-information tables.

Your rights

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 you may access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data, and object to its processing. To exercise these rights, contact the controller at the address above or email privacy@communitymediatrust.org.

Retention

We keep request, scorecard, and report data only as long as needed to deliver the service and meet legal obligations, after which it is deleted or anonymised.
